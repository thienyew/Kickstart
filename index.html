About

A studio that builds like a proper company should.

NoirTech is a web development studio in Petaling Jaya working with Malaysian small and mid-sized businesses. We do three things: company websites, online stores with real payment processing, and dashboards that turn scattered spreadsheets into decisions.

Most businesses here face a choice between agency rates that don't fit the budget, or freelancers who disappear after handover. We sit in between — production-quality work, properly documented and secured, at pricing that makes sense for a business your size.

You deal directly with the person building your project. No account managers, no work quietly passed to someone else.

Track recordDelivered a corporate site for a Bursa Malaysia-listed group, and a full e-commerce platform with live Malaysian payment processing.
PricingFixed quotes agreed before work begins. No hourly billing, no surprise invoices. High-quality work at reasonable rates.
CompliancePDPA-compliant data handling and security hardening included on every build — standard, not a paid extra.
